Inno & Leana

Game room

Three games for two phones. Pick one, then send the same link to the other person.

Truth or Dare

Toss a coin, then take turns asking. A truth earns 1 point, a dare earns 2, and chickening out costs you one. There's a chat built in.

Play Truth or Dare

ZIM8

The Zimbabwean Crazy Eights. Match the suit or number, stack your 2s and Jokers, and finish clean on a 3, 4, 5, 6, 9, 10 or Q.

Play ZIM8

Tsoro Yematatu

The three-stone game. Place your stones on the triangle, then slide them one step at a time until you get three in a row.

Play Tsoro

How to start a game

  1. Open a game on your phone.
  2. Tap your name. Inno or Leana, it remembers you after that.
  3. Send the same link to the other person on WhatsApp, and play.

Games save on your phone and update by themselves. Open links in Chrome rather than WhatsApp's own browser.
