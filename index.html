Game room

Games for two, even when you're apart. Make an account, invite your partner, and play live on your own phones.

Truth or Dare

Toss a coin, then take turns asking. A truth earns 1 point, a dare earns 2, and chickening out costs you one. There's a chat built in.

Play Truth or Dare

ZIM8

The Zimbabwean Crazy Eights. Match the suit or number, stack your 2s and Jokers, and finish clean on a 3, 4, 5, 6, 9, 10 or Q.

Play ZIM8

Tsoro Yematatu

The three-stone game. Place your stones on the triangle, then slide them one step at a time until you get three in a row.

Play Tsoro

Two Spies

A word game you play as a team. Give one-word clues that point to your secret agents, and find all 15 together in 9 turns without touching an assassin.

Play Two Spies

Snakes & Ladders

Roll the die and race to 100. Climb the ladders, dodge the snakes, roll a 6 to go again, and land on 100 exactly.

Play Snakes & Ladders

Tsoro yemakomba

A solo sowing puzzle on a stone board. Pick up balls, drop them round the loop, chain on, and gather every ball into one hole. Stuck? Press Solve.

Play Tsoro yemakomba

How to start a game

  1. Create an account with a username only you have.
  2. Invite your partner by their username. They accept on their phone.
  3. Both of you open the same game and play. Only the two of you can see it.

You stay signed in on your phone, so you only do this once. Games save and update by themselves. Open the site in Chrome rather than WhatsApp's own browser.
