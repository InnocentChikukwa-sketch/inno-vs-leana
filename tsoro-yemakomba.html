Tsoro yemakomba

Each side of the line is 2 rows × 4 holes and is its own puzzle. Tap a hole to pick up its balls; they drop one by one around that side (clockwise, the small numbers show the order). If your last ball lands in a hole that already has balls, pick them all up and keep going. Stop when it lands in an empty hole. Gather every ball of a side into one hole to win that side.
