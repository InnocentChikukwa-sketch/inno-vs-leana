Tsoro yemakomba

Two players, one each side of the line (2 rows × 4 holes), take turns. Each side has a red master hole: if your last ball lands there you can't pick up, and the other player plays. Tap a hole to pick up its balls; they drop one by one around that side (clockwise, the small numbers show the order). If your last ball lands in a hole that already has balls, pick them all up and keep going. If it lands in an empty hole, your turn passes. The first to gather every ball of their side into one hole wins.
